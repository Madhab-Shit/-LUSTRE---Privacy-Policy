Privacy Policy for LUSTRE

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

Application: LUSTRE (Package: com.sevendashers.shopcart)

Welcome to LUSTRE. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and the steps we take to safeguard your data in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.

1. Information We Collect

When you register, place orders, or interact with our application, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
  • Profile & Media Data: Profile photos or images you choose to upload for your profile.
  • Transaction & Order Data: Items added to your cart, wishlist, order history, payment mode (e.g., Cash on Delivery, Wallet points), and delivery details.
  • Device & Log Data: Device model, operating system version, and general diagnostic logs to ensure the stability and security of the application.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your personal user account.
  • To process, fulfill, and deliver your orders.
  • To calculate and manage your rewards and wallet balance (Beauty Coins).
  • To communicate with you regarding your orders, updates, and customer support inquiries.
  • To improve our services, user interface, and application security.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide high-quality services, LUSTRE integrates trusted third-party service providers who have access to data solely to perform specific tasks on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Google Cloud): Used for secure cloud database management (Cloud Firestore), user data storage, and authentication. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudinary: Used for secure image storage and media hosting. Cloudinary Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Security

We implement appropriate technical and security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active or as necessary to fulfill orders and provide services.

5. Account and Data Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play's User Data Policy, users have the right to request deletion of their account and all associated personal data at any time.

To request permanent deletion of your account and personal data:

  • Send an email to sevendashers@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Account - LUSTRE" from your registered email address.
  • Upon receipt of your request, your personal data, profile information, and order records will be permanently removed from our active database within 30 business days.

6. Children’s Privacy

LUSTRE does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it from our servers immediately.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
